COLLEGE TIMINGS AND WORKING HOURS
The college campus operates Monday through Friday between 8:30 AM and 5:00 PM.
College bus arrival: All college buses reach the campus by 9:10 AM.
Morning assembly: The morning student assembly commences promptly at 9:15 AM in the main quadrangle.
Staff attendance punch: The biometric punch-in deadline for faculty and staff members is 9:30 AM.
Standard classroom lectures commence after assembly at 9:30 AM and conclude at 4:15 PM.
The official lunch break is scheduled daily from 12:30 PM to 1:30 PM.
Administrative offices, fee counters, and the student affairs desk remain accessible from 9:00 AM to 4:30 PM.
The central library and computer labs remain open until 6:00 PM on all working days.

COLLEGE BUS AND TRANSPORTATION RULES
College buses depart from various designated pickup points across the city and arrive at the college gate at 9:10 AM.
Evening departure for all college buses is scheduled at 4:30 PM from the main bus bay.
Students using institution transport must carry their valid Bus Pass at all times and adhere to standard transport safety guidelines.

ATTENDANCE RULES AND REGULATIONS
Every student must maintain a minimum overall attendance of 75% in each registered course to be eligible for end-semester examinations.
Students with attendance between 65% and 74% due to verified medical emergencies or institutional duty representation may apply for condonation by submitting a medical certificate or duty leave form to the Dean of Academic Affairs.
A condonation fee of INR 1,000 per subject applies upon approval.
Students with attendance below 65% are strictly detained (Grade FA - Failure due to Attendance) and are disqualified from writing the final theory and practical examinations for that semester. Such students must re-register for the course in subsequent semesters.

GRADING SYSTEM AND EVALUATION
The college adopts a 10-point relative Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA) system.
Grade O (Outstanding): 90% and above (10 grade points).
Grade A+ (Excellent): 80% to 89% (9 grade points).
Grade A (Very Good): 70% to 79% (8 grade points).
Grade B+ (Good): 60% to 69% (7 grade points).
Grade B (Above Average): 50% to 59% (6 grade points).
Grade C (Pass): 40% to 49% (5 grade points).
Grade U (Re-appear / Fail): Below 40% (0 grade points).
Passing minimum: A student must secure a minimum of 40% in internal continuous assessments and 40% in the end-semester examination separately, with an aggregate score of at least 50% to pass the subject.

CURRICULUM AND SYLLABUS DETAILS (SEMESTER 5)
Course Code: CS501 - Database Management Systems (DBMS)
Credits: 4
Core Topics: Relational Data Models, SQL Queries, Normalization (1NF, 2NF, 3NF, BCNF), Transaction Management, ACID Properties, Concurrency Control, and Indexing Strategies.

Course Code: CS502 - Computer Networks (CN)
Credits: 4
Core Topics: OSI and TCP/IP Reference Models, Transmission Media, Data Link Layer Protocols, IPv4 and IPv6 Addressing, Routing Algorithms (Dijkstra, Distance Vector), TCP/UDP Transport Protocols, and Application Layer Protocols (HTTP, DNS, FTP).

Course Code: CS503 - Operating Systems (OS)
Credits: 3
Core Topics: Process Scheduling, Multi-threading, Process Synchronization (Semaphores, Mutex), Deadlock Detection and Avoidance, Memory Management (Paging, Segmentation, Virtual Memory), and File System Architecture.

Course Code: CS504 - Internet of Things and Embedded Systems (IoT)
Credits: 3
Core Topics: Microcontrollers (ESP32, Arduino architecture), Sensor Interfacing, Actuator Control, Wireless Protocols (Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, Zigbee), MQTT and HTTP Communication, Cloud Data Logging, and Automation Systems.

CAMPUS FACILITIES AND HOSTEL DISCIPLINE
Hostel in-times: All hostel resident students must return to their respective hostel blocks before 7:30 PM on weekdays and 8:30 PM on weekends.
Ragging and substance abuse are strictly prohibited under zero-tolerance state anti-ragging laws. Violations lead to immediate police reporting and expulsion.
Identity Cards must be worn visibly around the neck at all times while inside the college gates.
