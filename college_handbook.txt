COLLEGE ACADEMIC REGULATIONS & POLICIES

1. Attendance Policy:
- Minimum required attendance to be eligible for end-semester exams is 75%.
- Attendance between 65% and 74% can be condoned by the Dean upon submission of valid medical proof.
- Attendance below 65% is strictly not condoned; the student must re-register and repeat the course.

2. Grading System:
- Grade O (Outstanding): 90% and above (10 Grade Points)
- Grade A+ (Excellent): 80% to 89% (9 Grade Points)
- Grade A (Very Good): 70% to 79% (8 Grade Points)
- Grade B (Good): 60% to 69% (7 Grade Points)
- Minimum passing mark is 50% (Grade C).

3. Syllabus Details:
- CS301 (Data Structures): Arrays, Linked Lists, Stacks, Queues, Binary Trees, Graph Traversal (BFS/DFS), and Hashing.
- CS302 (Database Management Systems): ER Modeling, Relational Algebra, SQL Queries, Normalization (1NF, 2NF, 3NF, BCNF), and Transaction Processing.
